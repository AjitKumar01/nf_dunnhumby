What has been run, and what it establishes

The model is implemented and validated; it recovers its own parameters; two of its design claims are now tested and one of those tests reversed a result I had already reported. It has not been fitted to dunnhumby, and this page says exactly what stands between here and there.

Part II
The design claims, tested

3The size potential, and a result that reversed

Section 14 of the specification argues that basket-size dispersion must be carried by a free size potential rather than by the Gaussian latent, because a latent reaching dunnhumby's dispersion must operate where the estimator measurably breaks. The test is an ablation: hold ρ0 at zero and see what is lost.

The first run said the size potential was worthless, and the first run was under-powered

Generated truth with dispersion 0.525. Ablating ρ0 cost +0.0033 nats per basket — nothing — and the no-size fit actually produced a better basket-size distribution (TVD 0.0376 against 0.0499).

That is not evidence against the size potential. It is a test run in the regime where section 14 says the term is not needed. A dispersion of 0.525 is below the Poisson-binomial ceiling of 1, so the latent alone can reproduce it and the extra parameters only add variance. Reporting that number as an ablation result would have been wrong, and I nearly did.

Reaching a higher-dispersion truth took three failed attempts. A quadratic ρ0 tops out near 0.93 whatever its coefficients: it cannot make the size law bimodal, and conditioning on a non-empty basket removes the low mode that would supply the spread. The fix is to stop guessing the shape and solve for it — since P(n|z) ∝ e−ρ0(n)An(z), setting ρ0(n) = log An(0) − log target(n) makes the size law equal any chosen target at z = 0. Targeting a two-humped law — a small top-up trip or a large stock-up one — gives dispersion 1.335, above the ceiling a latent-only model can reach.

At dispersion above 1, the result reverses completely

  • Co-purchase φ is worth +0.3605 nats.
  • The mechanism is visible, and it is the one section 14 names: without a free size potential the co-purchase embedding is corrupted trying to carry the dispersion — φ Gram recovery falls from +0.8515 to +0.7549. The latent does not simply fail to represent size; it distorts the parameter that has to represent something else.
  • Basket-size TVD nearly doubles without it, 0.097 → 0.172.

Honest limit: dispersion 1.335 is still far below dunnhumby's 4.35. The direction and the mechanism are established; the magnitude at realistic dispersion is not.

4Forward-buying, in the real data

The specification removed an inventory state because dunnhumby records no inventory, and replaced it with a days-of-supply proxy whose only new content beyond recency is how much was bought last time. scripts/theory/test8_forward_buying.py asks whether that content exists: 797,533 category purchase occasions, within (household, category), household-clustered standard errors.

designresult
gap to next purchase on units taken, within (household, category)β = +0.01596 (se 0.00126, t = +12.65)
… also controlling for the trip's total sizeβ = +0.00973 (se 0.00119, t = +8.17)

… adding unitslog-loss 0.234416, a gain of 0.000387 nats

The sign is unambiguous and in the forward-buying direction; the size is small — one extra unit lengthens the next gap by 0.97%, or 0.41 days against a mean of 41.8. Held out, the gain is about a seventieth of the 0.028-nat effect this project has treated as evidence elsewhere.

Why the two designs disagree about importance

By a predictive standard the term does not earn its place. By a policy standard it does, and the distinction is not special pleading: a counterfactual uses the coefficient, not the log-loss. Units barely vary exogenously in observational data, so the predictive gain is small; a promotion deliberately moves them, and the same coefficient then applies to much larger variation.

The consequence for the environment is directional and should be stated plainly: promotional pull-forward is representable, and it is weak. A markdown policy evaluated here will see most of the sales lift and only a little of the payback.

5The phase transition, with the size potential active

scripts/theory/verify_aligned_worstcase.py runs the worst case for stability — every product loading the latent identically — over all 65,536 subsets, sweeping the loading a against a convex ρ0(n) = ρ n(n−1)/2.

ρfirst a with two modesat a = 0.60: modesESSlog Z error

It delays the transition; it does not abolish it. At a fixed loading the improvement is large — two modes and 0.68 nats of error become one mode and 0.003 — but push a far enough and it returns, and worse: at ρ = 0.10, a = 0.75 multi-start finds six modes, ESS 0.006, and log Z 10.3 nats out. A strong size penalty makes the failure rarer and more severe.

Two findings transfer regardless of the toy's small catalogue. The Laplace variance formula understates Var(n) at every sub-critical λ — so the loading needed to reach a given dispersion is lower than the formula says, not higher, which is the opposite of what an earlier draft asserted from a table of unsigned errors. And ESS can collapse without a bifurcation: at ρ = 0.10, a = 0.65 it falls to 0.065 with a single mode, so multi-start alone is not a sufficient diagnostic and both must be logged.

What is not done

7The gap between here and a dunnhumby result

Stated as work, not as caveats.

#whatwhy it is not donecost

Padding to the maximum is what made an earlier branch cost 17.8 hours instead of 1.68.the main engineering job

5The causal layer — the coupon design, the tier separation, the sensitivity band.every causal claim in the specification is untested. The coupon panel is built and the design is stated; nothing has been estimated.needs 3

What this page does not claim

That version 3 is better than version 2 on real data. Nothing here compares them on dunnhumby, and the one place they make conflicting predictions — the category-entry response, 1.243 against 0.858 — is untested and is the kind of disagreement that an architecture cannot tune its way out of.

What is established is narrower and worth having on its own: the mathematics is right, the implementation computes it, the estimator recovers what generated the data, and two of the design's own claims have been tested rather than asserted — one of which reversed a result already reported here.
